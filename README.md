📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes sales, customer behavior, product performance, and city-level revenue for Monday Coffee, an online coffee retailer operating across multiple Indian cities since 2023.

The objective is to use SQL-based analysis to:

Understand revenue drivers

Analyze customer purchasing behavior

Evaluate product performance across cities

Study revenue trends and growth

Identify cities with high market potential for expansion

All analysis is performed using relational SQL only, without modifying the original data structure.

🗂️ Database Schema

The analysis is based on four relational tables:

city – city-level demographic and rent data

customers – customer details mapped to cities

products – coffee product catalog

sales – transactional sales data

Tables are connected using primary and foreign keys, enabling multi-level business analysis.

🔍 Business Questions Answered
1. City-wise Revenue Contribution

Which cities generate the highest revenue, and what percentage of total revenue does each city contribute?

2. Top Customers by Lifetime Value

Who are the top revenue-generating customers based on total historical purchases?

3. Customer Purchase Behavior

How many customers are one-time buyers versus repeat customers?

4. Monthly Revenue Trend

How does total revenue change month over month across the business?

5. Month-over-Month Revenue Growth

What is the percentage growth or decline in revenue compared to the previous month?

6. Product Performance Analysis

Which products are ordered most frequently across all cities?

7. Top 3 Products by City

What are the top three products in each city based on order volume?

8. Average Revenue per Customer by City

Which cities have customers with higher average spending?

9. Market Potential Assessment

How does the number of actual customers compare with the estimated coffee-consuming population (assumed as 25% of total population)?

10. Low-Performing City Identification

Which cities have customers but relatively low total revenue, indicating underperformance?


📈 Key Insights

Revenue is highly concentrated in a small number of cities

Repeat customers contribute significantly more revenue than one-time buyers

Certain cities show a large gap between estimated coffee consumers and actual customers, indicating strong expansion potential

Product demand varies significantly by city, supporting localized inventory planning

Some cities have customers but low revenue, highlighting opportunities for targeted marketing or pricing optimization
